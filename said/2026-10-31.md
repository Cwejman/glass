## 1. Written for the grant

What I wrote answering the Foresight Institute, kept as it was written.

### 1.1 What I am trying to do

A new foundation for computing. All data, programs and screens live in one shared field of small, whole pieces. Programs find the data they fit, every action happens visibly in the field, and interfaces assemble themselves. AI works inside it directly, without the layers of glue software needed today.

### 1.2 What is new

Today AI reaches into computing through harnesses: sessions, prompts, tool wrappers, permission dialogs and cloud APIs, each making up for primitives that are missing. Data is locked inside apps, context is rebuilt for every session, and safety is policed from outside.

What is new is a simple set of primitives. Data, types, programs, AI and interfaces are one field of whole pieces. Programs match the data they fit by type, and every effect passes through the field, visible and bounded. Interfaces compose themselves the same way.

### 1.3 Safety first

Advancing safety first is the natural order here, because the field abstracts the layer that does the compute. Its boundaries are the first thing built. Everything starts in local trust: the field, the engine and the interface are local, and programs execute in protected environments, such as isolated VMs. Every program, completion model execution included, acts only through the field, where each effect is visible and each boundary is strictly applied. Transparency and sovereignty come from the primitives themselves, so they come first.

### 1.4 AI in the field

AI is just another program in the field, governed like every program: every action is visible and boundaries are strictly enforced, so security becomes structural. Sessions no longer exist; each inference assembles its context from the field, so the field is what you cache as close to the hardware as possible. Local compute is its natural home. We build the local-first foundation first, usable at once, then bring it ever closer to the hardware, where inference truly happens.

### 1.5 Compounding

The value is that everything compounds, because the field is unified. As Unix did with a few pure primitives, this gives ownership, transparency and a simple way for things to build on each other. Compounding begins with the first milestone. The foundation is useful on its own, before there is much else to build on.
