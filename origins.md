# Where it comes from

Glass comes from two of the author's works and one attempt of its own. OpenLight gave the vision and most of the architecture, the rule gave the shape knowledge takes, and glass's first iteration, built and cleared in three days, showed what building ahead of the architecture costs.

*The author's, 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-01.*

## 1. OpenLight

[OpenLight](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight) claims that completion should happen from a point in a field, not from a pasted transcript. When knowledge, programs and interfaces live as typed, versioned structure in one medium, programs compound, interfaces build on primitives as data does, and the field keeps everything. Its specs, many hours of thinking about what is wanted, name the engine, the chassis, the view and the reader that glass carries.

Glass is its successor, written in a different spaciousness. The author began OpenLight with a context window of at most two hundred thousand tokens and very different models; writing it, he met many details that do not matter. Glass assembles a core set of primitives, done with quality, aligned with OpenLight's vision.

*The author's, 2026-10-30, 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01; OpenLight's claim carried from its README.*

### 1.1 What glass keeps

The engine as its own artefact, with one protocol as its only contract. The chassis that hosts and decides nothing. The interface that holds no lasting state, with components declaring what they take and mounts as calls. The home as a store, with what it attaches and the entry it boots into. Programs and places in place of chrome. The reader component as what is wanted.

*Read in OpenLight's specs, 2026-11-01.*

### 1.2 What is new

OpenLight's pilot was a desktop window, with no address bar; glass is first a client in a browser, so the URL needs an answer. The store as a remote was on OpenLight's horizon, as the object model to come; glass begins with it. And knowledge, which OpenLight deferred, comes first, laid by the gradient the rule found after OpenLight's specs were written.

*Reasoned, the session's, 2026-11-01; the URL as new the author's.*

## 2. The rule

[The rule](https://github.com/Cwejman/the-rule) is the author's law for knowledge: the biggest understanding first, each breaking down into smaller ones, as briefs in a holarchy joined by links. Its last section reaches past prose, to a medium where programs meet by matching, a model's context is a selection from the medium, and the harness dissolves, and it names OpenLight as where that is pursued.

This wiki is written under the rule, and the store's first content is knowledge, so glass is also where the rule's medium is first lived beyond markdown and git.

*Carried from the rule, sections 1, 3 and 6; the rule as how we embody things the author's, 2026-10-30.*

## 3. Glass's first iteration

From 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-01 glass was built ahead of its architecture: a SQLite field, an engine that was also the web server, Solid components drawing the field, every visit kept as a holon, editing in place, and runs. Much of it was faked until made, and on 2026-11-01 it was cleared, kept in this repository's git history rather than carried.

What it showed is in this wiki, each part as the decision it led to. The engine served pages and spoke to the interface directly, with no protocol between them, and now the client holds the engine. Runs were written into the field that would be published, and now they belong to the home. Choosing another component rewrote the published layout, and now layout is partitioned to the reader. And tabs and views were added that the author never asked for.

*The author's, 2026-11-01: a lot about glass is fake till you make it, and the tabs he never asked for; the lessons drawn are the session's reading of what was built.*
