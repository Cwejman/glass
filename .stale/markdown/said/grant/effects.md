# AI in the field

AI is just another program in the field, governed like every program: every action is visible and boundaries are strictly enforced, so security becomes structural. Sessions no longer exist; each inference assembles its context from the field, so the field is what you cache as close to the hardware as possible. Local compute is its natural home. We build the local-first foundation first, usable at once, then bring it ever closer to the hardware, where inference truly happens.
