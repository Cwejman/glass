# The site

Every holon gets a page of its own, at the path it stands at: the bar, the path down from the root, the holon whole, what stands beneath it as a field, what speaks of it, and last the way into it as data. The root's page is the same arrangement, which is why it is one screen: the root, and everything beneath it at once.
