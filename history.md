## Relations are settled, and a holon is part of the field

On 2026-11-02 the author read the primitives and the store and settled what a store holds and how holons relate. It was the longest day of thinking so far, about 2,300 of his words, and nothing was built.

### The store's objects are settled

Reading the store, the author took the `by` out of a commit: a change is made by a program's run, and the run records the commits it made, so the link runs from the run and the commit needs no author. He asked for an index that does not bloat, and it was split by id so a change rewrites one root and one part. A version became what the store needs and then the holon's body, with title and prose inside the body.

[A commit holds its parent, its index and its time; the run records the commits it made](git:6ab1043)

### How holons relate

The author asked whether OpenLight's relates was worth anything, and then asked the same of ownership. A first run of subagents went ahead of him and away from what he could follow, so he forked the session back to before it and reasoned it through himself: OpenLight is built bottom up, knowledge either explains its connections in prose or has typed fields, and namespace is only the possibility of where.

He then asked for wide simulations, and six were run, each a whole field across stores rather than one case at a time. They converged: a holon stores its identity, what it is an instance of and its body; every other relation is a reference in a body, seen without types; whoever can write holds the relation, which across stores is always one's own home; and part and whole are read from the whole's body. Ownership, relates and a universal membership were let go. One whole simulated field was written out as chunk-script data for him to read, where he renamed a run's `came` to its result.

[How holons relate: identity, instance and body; references in bodies; part and whole read from the whole](git:faf9558)

The simulated field he read stands beside the wiki as [a sketch](sketches/relations.html).

### A holon is part of the field

The relations raised what makes a holon whole once part and whole are read from the whole. The author answered from Koestler: everything is part of the field. The primitive now says a holon is whole in itself, and part of the field and of any whole that holds it, and nothing else needed changing.

[A holon is part of the field, and of any whole that holds it](git:8bc2f96)
