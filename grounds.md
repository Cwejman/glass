# The grounds

Glass rests on what matters most to it, and what decides that is its ontology: what exists in it, held as knowledge in language that a knowledge graph can be built on. That is the primitives. Everything else keeps, runs or draws them, and each part should be as separate as the primitives it serves, and read as what it is.

*The author's, [2026-11-02](said/2026-11-02.md#11-the-grounds), given as a little philosophy for the culture glass grows in; the wording the session's.*

## 1. What matters is the potential

Asking what truly matters, and what potential it has, gives ground to rest on. That ground is understood by what is most decisive for it, its ontology: what exists, said as knowledge in language, so that a graph of knowledge can be built on it. In glass that is [the primitives](primitives.md).

So once a question is settled, as the store's objects and the relations now are, attention goes back from it to the grounds, rather than on to more of the same detail.

*The author's, 2026-11-02.*

## 2. Each layer stands apart

The store is separate. It is there to be read, and is honoured as data: static, every state a snapshot that never changes, readable by anything that reads files.

The engine stands on it, and is what makes the field testable: every act passes through it, so every act can be tested there, at its own layer, without an interface.

Interfaces stand on the engine. Each is coupled only to itself and to the holons it takes, and decoupled from the others by the space between the primitives, so together they are a well-made set and not one whole that has to move at once.

*The author's, 2026-11-02; that the engine is testable because every act passes through it the session's reading, from [the engine](primitives.md#4-engine).*

## 3. The code carries the tone

The tone, register and nuance in which glass is understood is how its code looks, how it is to use, and how its pieces compose. The architecture reflects the ontology of the primitives, and that reflection carries on outward into everything made: the data, the contracts types and the protocol set, and the code. Code that reads like the primitives it serves is the culture kept.

*The author's, 2026-11-02; the contracts named as types' and the protocol's the session's reading.*

The primitives are built to make a world of programs, and a program and its code are not always the same unit. Descending into the code, it is looked upon as artfully as anything else built on the primitives, for what it shows of the archetypal foundations of building applications.

*The author's, [2026-11-02](said/2026-11-02.md#13-fields-as-connectors).*

## 4. Three notions, one ontology

Glass is three notions meeting: ontology, pure functional compute, and language models. Inference chooses, pure compute acts, and both work on one ontology: a field's shape lets a program compute over a holon, and its prose lets a model understand the same holon, so a model can choose and compose programs by what they mean, and the programs then run with no inference in them at all.

What it serves is giving and receiving. Good compute, technology, interfaces and connections go both ways: from the person using it, to the group, to the greater whole.

*The three notions and both ways the author's, [2026-11-02](said/2026-11-02.md#14-both-ways); inference choosing and pure compute acting on one ontology the session's words, which the author took as his.*
